What We Do?

At Reach, we recognize the multi-faceted nature of food insecurity and are dedicated to implementing impactful initiatives that address this critical issue. Through collaborative efforts, strategic partnerships, and innovative programs, we strive to provide sustainable solutions and support to individuals and families facing food insecurity, irrespective of their employment status or background.

Hunger Report offers a thought-provoking overview of the prevalent issue of food insecurity within Australia. The report delves into the landscape of food poverty across our nation, shedding light on its impact on various segments of the population. In particular, this information brings attention to the pressing concern of child hunger in Australia.

Outlined below are key facts and statistics highlighting the gravity of the hunger crisis in our country during 2022:

In Australia, the issue of food insecurity affects a substantial number of individuals and spans across diverse segments of the population.

  • Recent data reveals that approximately 3.6 million Australians, constituting 15% of the population, have encountered food insecurity at least once within the past 12 months.
  • Remarkably, three in five of these individuals face recurring episodes of food insecurity on a monthly basis.

It is important to note that food insecurity extends beyond traditional stereotypes and is not limited to the unemployed or homeless. This highlights the pervasive nature of the problem and emphasizes the need for comprehensive solutions that address the diverse circumstances in which individuals experience food insecurity.

Youngest community members are not immune to the impacts of food insecurity. Dependent children reside in 40% of households affected by food insecurity, underlining the urgent need to safeguard the well-being and future of our future generations.By presenting this data, our aim is to raise awareness and initiate constructive discussions about the urgent need to address food insecurity at its core.

Hunger Report serves as a crucial tool in advocating for effective policies, interventions, and collaborative efforts to combat hunger and ensure a brighter future for every individual in our community.Together, let us combat food insecurity and build a society where every Australian has access to nutritious and adequate food resources. Join us in making a difference and creating a brighter future for all members of our community.
