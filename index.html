How we make a difference

Reach is a NGO speaking up for and supporting regional communities.

Who we are

We work to create thriving, inclusive and economically active regional communities.“We believe no one should be disadvantaged because of where they live.”.

At Reach we support our partners to deliver initiatives that equip people with the knowledge, skills, and connections needed to improve their community

Mission

Empowering lives through safe housing, crisis support, compassionate case management, food assistance, employment help, and connecting with NDIS and Aged Care, while combating domestic violence and fostering community support.

Vision

To Empower individuals and families with secure housing, freedom from violence, comprehensive support, and connections to NDIS and Aged Care, rebuilding lives with dignity, hope, and essential resources.
